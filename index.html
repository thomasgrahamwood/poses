Before a nude session, agree on boundaries in writing: what will be shown, who is on set, where the images go. A closed set and a robe between setups make everyone more comfortable.
How to use itSave 8 to 12 poses before the shoot. Show the figure to your subject rather than describing it: people copy shapes faster than instructions.
Reading the figuresHover a pose to see what to emphasise, the idea behind it and the feeling it should give. Darker limbs sit behind the body. Use Mirror when your light comes from the other side.
